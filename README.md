## 🧭 What Is Atlas Sanctum?

**Atlas Sanctum** is a regenerative intelligence platform connecting **data, governance, capital, and action** to help communities understand complex realities and turn intelligence into measurable regeneration.

The **Living Reality Dashboard** is the platform's situational-awareness layer.

It provides a unified view of:

* Human wellbeing
* Ecological conditions
* Economic activity
* Infrastructure
* Health
* Food systems
* Migration
* Education
* Governance
* Social trust
* Climate and environmental signals
* Community-generated intelligence

The goal is not simply to display more data.

The goal is to connect:

```text
REALITY
   ↓
UNDERSTANDING
   ↓
INTELLIGENCE
   ↓
DECISION
   ↓
ACTION
   ↓
IMPACT
   ↓
REGENERATION
```

---

# 🧠 Product Philosophy

Atlas Sanctum is designed around a simple proposition:

> **A civilization cannot regenerate what it cannot see, understand, coordinate, and act upon.**

The Living Reality Dashboard therefore treats the world as an interconnected system rather than a collection of isolated metrics.

A change in food security can affect health.

A climate event can affect migration.

Migration can affect infrastructure.

Infrastructure can affect economic opportunity.

Economic opportunity can affect wellbeing.

The platform is designed to make these relationships more visible.

### Alert hierarchy

| Level          | Meaning                              |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 🔴 Critical    | Immediate or rapidly escalating risk |
| 🟠 Warning     | Significant developing condition     |
| 🟢 Normal      | Stable operating state               |
| 🔵 Information | Relevant contextual signal           |

Every production alert should ideally include:

```text
What happened?
Where?
When?
Who / what may be affected?
What evidence supports the alert?
How confident is the signal?
What action options exist?
```

# 🔐 Trust, Governance & Data Provenance

A planetary intelligence platform must make its evidence inspectable.

Production implementations should expose:

* Data source
* Collection time
* Geographic scope
* Update frequency
* Transformation history
* Model version
* Methodology
* Confidence / uncertainty
* Known limitations
* Access permissions

Important indicators should never become authoritative simply because they appear on a dashboard.
